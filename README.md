**The Midnight Gallery — a quiet little heist.** Thief Vesper steals the Star of Meridian from a museum gallery at night: sneak across one 1280×720 room past 3 patrolling guards (Mara, Dario, Ilsa) with lantern-light vision cones and 2 sweeping wall cameras with red cones, grab the glowing gem from its pedestal, and reach the green EXIT door. Being seen fills the live alarm meter — full means caught. Hold Shift to sneak (slower, harder to spot), click to toss a coin (×3) and lure the nearest guard away. Title / play / win / caught screens, timer, loot and coin counters. Vanilla JS canvas, no network requests, no vendored libraries.

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static HTTP server and open `index.html` in desktop Chrome. Keyboard: WASD / arrows to move, Shift to sneak, R to restart. Mouse: click to toss a coin.

## Hand it in

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Copies `game/` + `screenshots/` to the output directory and writes `dossier.json`.
